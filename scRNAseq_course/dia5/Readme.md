# DIA 5. Downstream Analysis of scRNAseq data

**Servicio de Análisis Biocomputacional (SABio); CBM**\
**Edition**: May, 2026\
**Last update**: 11/05/2026

------------------------------------------------------------------------

The materials included here focus on downstream biological interpretation of scRNA-seq datasets, covering several key analytical approaches commonly used in modern single-cell studies:

-   **Differential expression analysis** to identify marker genes and transcriptional differences between cell populations. [Folder: `5.1.Differential_Expression`]
-   **Differential proportion analysis** to compare cellular composition across samples or experimental conditions. [Folder: `5.2.Differential_Proportion`]
-   **Trajectory and pseudotime analysis** to reconstruct dynamic cellular processes such as differentiation or activation. [Folder: `5.3.Trajectory`]
-   **Cell-cell communication analysis** to infer signaling interactions between cell populations using ligand-receptor databases. [Folder: `5.4.Cell_Comunication`]
